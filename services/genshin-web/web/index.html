Team optimizer

Find your strongest teams

Tell the optimizer which characters, weapons and artifacts you own. It simulates every team you can build and picks the best set, with no character or weapon used twice.

Import a GOOD file from a scanner, or tap your characters and weapons in by hand. You can mix both.

Pick how generous energy drops are, and characters to always include or leave out.

Seven teams, one per element, or five of any element. Ranked by your weakest team or your average.

Why are the numbers different from my damage in game?

The simulator plays each team the same way every time, against one standard target, with the assumptions above. It is built to compare teams fairly, not to match a single run.

Where do the rotations come from?

The KQM Sim Database and KQM guides, credited on every team. Teams the database lacks get rotations from trusted guides.

Quick or refined?

Quick simulates every team you can field in minutes. Refined starts with a quick run, then simulates the most promising teams again: KQM Sim's substat optimiser picks substats for your weapons and constellations, and each team runs 250 times. About 120 teams are refined: the quick run's picks, the best of each element and each character's best team. The quick teams show while you wait.
